# Numberpad

## Overview 
<img width="418" height="513" alt="Screenshot 2026-11-11 at 12 46 30 PM" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/519b1150-e749-4074-a86f-aa294263a5d7" />
Behold! It is a numerical keypad that one may utilize to transcribe numerals. This splendiferous device will ensure that you can type as many numbers as your heart desires.

I designed this for my Week 5 project of halflife, a 10-week program where you can design and build hardware projects free of cost!

## Parts
* XIAO-nRF52840-SMD as the main microcontroller
* Four test points 
* 14 standard buttons and 3 big buttons to tap tap tap
* Diodes to prevent ghosting
